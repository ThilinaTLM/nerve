Make Nerve look and fit the way you like: choose a theme, set the zoom, and arrange your panels.

## Change the theme

1. Open **Settings → Workbench → Appearance**.
2. Choose a **Theme**: Nerve Light, Nerve Dark (the default), Melange Light, Melange Dark, or Graphite.

There is no separate light/dark or system setting; each theme is a complete look. Your choice is saved in the browser or window you are using, so two devices connected to the same daemon can use different themes.

## Zoom

Press `Mod+=` to zoom in, `Mod+-` to zoom out, and `Mod+0` to reset. Zoom moves in fixed steps and applies to the whole workbench. On small screens, zoom does not replace the compact layout.

## Panels and layout

Panel positions, sizes, visibility, and active tabs are remembered per browser, so another browser starts with its own layout.

Below 1024 px wide, use the sheet controls to open dock content. On phones, spacing tightens further and popovers open as bottom sheets you can pull down to dismiss.

## Sounds and notifications

Sounds, toasts, and desktop notifications are covered in [Notifications and activity](/guides/notifications/). Their preferences live in **Settings → Notifications**.

## Good to know

- Shortcuts are fixed and can't be remapped. See the [shortcut reference](/reference/shortcuts/).
- Retired themes switch to a current theme automatically.
